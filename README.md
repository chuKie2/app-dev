# PK (2014) 

## About the Movie

**PK** is a 2014 Indian science-fiction comedy-drama film directed by *Rajkumar Hirani* and starring **Aamir Khan**.

The story follows an alien named PK who arrives on Earth for research but loses the device he needs to communicate with his spaceship.

## Plot Summary

PK is an alien who tries to understand human behavior, culture, and religion. Since he is unfamiliar with Earth's traditions, he asks unusual questions that make people rethink their beliefs.

Along his journey, he meets people who teach him about:

- **Friendship** – Building meaningful relationships with others.
- **Love** – Understanding emotions and caring for someone.
- **Religion** – Questioning traditions and understanding faith.
- **Humanity** – Treating everyone with kindness and respect.

## Why I Like This Movie 

I like PK because it is both entertaining and meaningful. It uses comedy to discuss serious topics and encourages viewers to think critically.

My favorite part is how PK questions things that people normally accept without understanding.

> "Wrong number!"
